Baritone Bot

A graphical control panel for Baritone in Minecraft. Instead of typing #goto 100 64 200 or #mine diamond_ore by hand, press a key and click through a tabbed menu.

Features

Press B (rebindable under Options > Controls > Key Binds > Baritone UI) to open the panel.

Tab	What it does
Movement	Go to coordinates, path to the block under your cursor, come to you, follow an entity or players, walk in your facing direction, explore, go to the surface
Mining	Mine chosen blocks, farm, stop farming, blacklist the current target, click a block to pick a target
Building	Build a schematic, plus selection tools: set positions, clear, fill, copy, paste
Waypoints	Set and go to a home, save, go to, list, or clear waypoints
Settings	View and change Baritone settings
Console	Send any Baritone command and see recent history, plus Help, Version, and Render shortcuts

Pause, Resume, and Cancel/Stop buttons are always visible at the bottom of the panel.
